About Us

A foundation built to open doors for grassroots children

The Joshua Ogundipe Foundation (JOF) is a non-profit organization committed to expanding access to quality education and creating opportunities for children and young people, particularly at the grassroots level. We believe that every child — regardless of background or socioeconomic status — deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and realize their full potential.

Through educational support, mentorship, advocacy, community engagement, and capacity-building initiatives, we work to inspire hope, nurture talent, and empower the next generation of leaders — partnering with schools, families, community leaders, volunteers, government agencies, and other development organizations to build sustainable solutions.

What drives us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Vision

Equal access, for every child

To create a society where every child has equal access to quality education, the opportunity to succeed, and the ability to contribute meaningfully to their community and the world.

Our Mission

Breaking barriers to learning

To transform lives by breaking barriers to learning, empowering boys and girls in grassroots communities through education, mentorship, advocacy, and community-driven initiatives — while promoting equity, excellence, and sustainable development.

Our mission extends beyond education. We strive to foster inclusive communities where children and young people can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally — partnering with schools, families, community leaders, volunteers, government agencies, and other development organizations to create sustainable solutions that improve educational outcomes and promote lifelong learning. We firmly believe that meaningful change is achieved through collaboration, innovation, and hard work.
